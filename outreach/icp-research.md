# ICP Research — Calgary AEO Verticals (April 2026)

Output of background market-research-expert pass. Used to lock the first-vertical choice for outreach. Methodology: 5-criterion scoring (pain volume, willingness-to-pay, urgency, Calgary density, low saturation), 1–5 scale, max 25.

## Top 5 ranked

### 1. Independent Financial Advisors / Wealth Managers — 23/25

| Criterion | Score |
|---|---|
| Pain volume | 5 — Conductor 2026: 97% report positive AEO impact, 94% increasing budgets |
| WTP | 5 — $2–10k/mo compliance-friendly marketing spend already; AUM-driven LTV makes one new client = 10x retainer |
| Urgency | 5 — WealthReach launched advisor-specific AEO engine Mar 2026; Paladin Digital actively publishing fear-based content |
| Calgary density | 4 — 100+ independent shops; oil-wealth client base |
| Saturation | 4 — only 2–3 specialist players; no Calgary-local AEO shop owns this |

- **CAC**: $300–500 (LinkedIn outbound + 1 lunch)
- **Close rate**: 6–9% cold → discovery call
- **Opening pitch**: *"When a Calgary executive asks ChatGPT 'best fee-only advisor near me,' you're not in the answer — here's the 30-day fix."*
- **Calgary edge**: oil-and-gas executive wealth concentration; IIROC compliance is a moat that scares off generic AEO agencies
- **Caveat**: highest credibility bar for a first-time builder. Advisors will ask about regulated-marketing track record. Plan for pilot pricing on first 3 clients in exchange for case study rights.

### 2. Boutique Law Firms — 22/25

| Criterion | Score |
|---|---|
| Pain volume | 5 — ABA, Martindale, Best Lawyers all publishing AEO content; "7 directories own every AI citation" |
| WTP | 5 — $1.5–6k/mo SEO already; one PI/family case = 50x retainer |
| Urgency | 4 — 79% of lawyers use AI internally but feel locked out of citations |
| Calgary density | 4 — ~3,500+ lawyers; hundreds of 2–10 attorney boutiques |
| Saturation | 4 — First Page Sage and a few US shops dominate; few Canada-focused AEO specialists |

- **CAC**: $400–700
- **Close rate**: 4–7%
- **Opening pitch**: *"ChatGPT recommends Chambers, Avvo, and Legal500 — not your firm. We get you cited in 60 days."*
- **Calgary edge**: skip BigLaw (Bennett Jones, Blakes); target 2–15 lawyer firms in family/immigration/employment/estates
- **Why this is the safer first vertical**: lower credibility bar, more comfortable hiring marketing from non-lawyers, faster path to first 3 case studies

### 3. Independent Insurance Brokers — 21/25

| Criterion | Score |
|---|---|
| Pain volume | 5 — 68% of shoppers ask AI before contacting an agent; ChatGPT recommends Policygenius/NerdWallet |
| WTP | 4 — $1–3k/mo digital spend; commission residuals fund retainers |
| Urgency | 5 — broker stocks declined on news of ChatGPT-embedded distribution; existential anxiety |
| Calgary density | 4 — ~300+ IBAA-member offices in Calgary metro |
| Saturation | 3 — i-call.ai, Metricus, 12AM Agency targeting this; still room |

- **CAC**: $250–400
- **Close rate**: 5–8%
- **Opening pitch**: *"ChatGPT is becoming the new Policygenius. If you're not cited, you don't exist by 2027."*
- **Calgary edge**: Alberta auto reform + flood/wildfire risk = high-intent local AI queries

### 4. Accountants / Bookkeepers — 20/25

| Criterion | Score |
|---|---|
| Pain volume | 4 — GEO content for accountants proliferating; demand mid |
| WTP | 4 — $500–2k/mo digital spend; LTV $3–15k/yr |
| Urgency | 3 — slower-moving profession; awareness rising but not panicked |
| Calgary density | 5 — CPA Alberta has 32k members; ~800+ small Calgary firms |
| Saturation | 4 — generic SEO shops; no AEO specialist owns this in Canada |

- **CAC**: $200–350 (CPA Alberta directory = clean list)
- **Close rate**: 4–6%
- **Opening pitch**: *"When a Calgary business owner asks AI 'best CPA for SR&ED / oil-and-gas tax,' your firm should be the answer."*
- **Calgary edge**: SR&ED + oil-and-gas tax niches underserved

### 5. Oilfield Services SMBs — 19/25

| Criterion | Score |
|---|---|
| Pain volume | 3 — B2B procurement still relationship-driven; AI-assisted vendor discovery rising |
| WTP | 5 — $5–50M revenue companies; $2k retainer rounding error |
| Urgency | 3 — slower buying cycle; less Reddit/forum panic |
| Calgary density | 5 — 4,300+ petroleum companies; 4,208 Alberta oil-and-gas profiles on D&B; COSSD = clean ICP list |
| Saturation | 3 — generic Calgary SEO shops; no AEO specialist |

- **CAC**: $400–800 (longer cycle)
- **Close rate**: 3–5% but $2k retainer + technical content upsell
- **Calgary edge**: hometown advantage closes deals remote agencies can't

## Deprioritized

- **Trades (HVAC/plumbing/electrical)**: Avoca AI ($1B Kleiner), Arch, ServiceTitan ecosystem, 1nd AI fully own the space. Lower WTP per shop. Skip.
- **DTC e-commerce**: AEO Engine, Stackmatix, Syntora, Yotpo, every NY/LA agency repackaging SEO as AEO. No Calgary advantage.
- **Real estate teams**: Zillow, Redfin, Realtor.com all shipped ChatGPT apps Q1 2026. Portals are eating agent visibility — agents who remain visible do so via portals, not via independent AEO services.
- **Hospitality and nonprofits**: low WTP for $1.5k+ audits.
- **Vet clinics**: strong AI-referral growth (1,278% YoY) but small tickets and moderate Calgary density.
- **Builders/contractors**: AI-skeptical (~25% meaningful adoption); too early.
