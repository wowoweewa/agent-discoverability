# Scoping Framework — AI Service Productization

Reusable template for evaluating any AI service opportunity. Apply when scoping a new offer, validating an existing one, or onboarding a client whose workflow we'd wrap.

## The eight questions

For one workflow a business repeats weekly:

1. **Who owns it now** — current human owner, role, time per week
2. **Inputs they collect** — what they need before starting
3. **Output they need** — what counts as "done"
4. **What breaks most often** — recurring failure mode that creates the pain
5. **What must stay human-reviewed** — judgment calls, regulated content, brand voice
6. **Assisted version** — agent drafts/analyzes; client does the work
7. **Managed version** — agent + operator do the work; client signs off
8. **Proof needed to trust it** — what evidence converts skeptics into buyers

## Why two tiers and not three

Three tiers (basic / pro / enterprise) is a SaaS pricing reflex that rarely fits services. Two tiers force a real distinction: *assisted* gives them an answer, *managed* delivers the outcome. Skipping a middle tier removes the indecision tax.

## Applied to Agent Discoverability audits

The weekly workflow being wrapped: a business's check on whether ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini are recommending them when potential customers ask category-relevant questions.

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| **Who owns it now** | Marketing manager, SEO agency, or no one (most common). 0–4 hrs/week if attempted. |
| **Inputs they collect** | Site URL, competitor list, brand and category keywords, target geography |
| **Output they need** | Per-LLM citation status, prioritized fix list, monthly delta vs. baseline |
| **What breaks most often** | Schema decay after CMS changes; content extraction failures after redesign; new LLM crawler rules they didn't know about; competitors out-publishing them |
| **What must stay human-reviewed** | Brand voice in any rebuild work; regulated-industry content (legal, financial, healthcare disclaimers); claims accuracy in cited content |
| **The audit** | Free. Full 7-pillar audit, prompt panel run, prioritized fix list, implementation path options. |
| **Ongoing tracking** | Monthly citation tracking across 4 LLMs, delta report, advisory. Client implements fixes. Scoped per engagement; no published price. |
| **Implementation engagement** | Scoped per engagement (one-time build for clients who want operator-implemented changes) |
| **Proof needed to trust** | Case studies showing citation-share lift over 60–90 days; verbatim before/after LLM responses; verifiable schema audit logs; testimonials from same vertical |

## How this maps to the offer

- **The audit (free)** = trial of the methodology; opens a consulting conversation
- **Ongoing tracking** = recurring citation tracking + delta report + advisory; client implements
- **Implementation engagement** = scoped one-time build for clients who want operator-implemented changes

No price is published for any of these. The audit is free, and anything after it is scoped in conversation.

## How to use this framework

When considering a new vertical or product expansion, fill in all eight rows before writing a landing page or pricing. If any row is blank or speculative, the offer isn't ready. If the proof column is empty, the first 3–5 clients are the proof — discount aggressively to acquire them in exchange for case study rights.
